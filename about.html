About

I'm swtrzbuilds — a builder who turns ideas into things people can actually use.

I build websites, experiment with products, and turn random ideas into real projects. I care about clean design, simple experiences, and creating things that have a reason to exist — not just things that look good in a screenshot.

Most of what I build starts the same way: a dumb-sounding question that won't leave me alone until I've built something to answer it. Rate My Username started as exactly that — a joke with a friend that turned into a live product with a database, user accounts, and a leaderboard people actually check back on. I'd rather ship something small and real than plan something big and theoretical.

How I work

I work solo, end to end — design, frontend, backend, and the copy that makes a product feel like it has a personality instead of just a function. I lean on AI tools heavily to move fast through implementation, but every product decision — what a feature should actually do, how it should feel, and when something is funny versus just mean — is mine to make. I think that distinction matters more as AI-assisted building becomes normal: the tools change, but the taste still has to come from somewhere.

I write up what I build as case studies, not just a project list — the decisions, the dead ends, and the specific bugs I had to fix — because I think the "how" is usually more interesting than the finished screenshot.
