03 — skills

What I'm good at

I'm a solo builder, so "skills" here means the full stack of what it actually takes to get an idea from a half-formed thought to something live that strangers use — not just the technical part. Below is what I lean on most, with notes on how each one actually showed up in shipping Rate My Username.

01

AI-Assisted Development

Using AI tools fluently and deliberately to go from idea to working product fast, without losing control over quality or taste. The tools handle the typing; every decision about what the product should actually do still runs through me, line by line.

02

Prompt Engineering

Getting precise, reliable output from AI models by structuring context, constraints, and iteration well. The scoring system behind Rate My Username lives or dies on this — a funny one-off result is easy, a consistent one across thousands of runs is the real problem.

03

Rapid Prototyping & Shipping

Taking a half-formed idea to something live in days, not months — then improving it in public based on what real people do with it. I'd rather launch a smaller version and learn from real usage than polish something nobody's touched yet.

04

Product & UX Thinking

Shaping ideas into products people actually want to click, share, and come back to. That means deciding what not to build as much as what to — the leaderboard, battles, and bracket mode all got added after watching real usage, not from a roadmap written in advance.

05

Web Development

Building and shipping real, live websites — from front-end polish to the backend handling thousands of real requests, to the unglamorous parts like rate limiting and edge-case handling that only matter once actual traffic shows up.

06

Voice & Copywriting

Writing copy with a point of view, so a result feels like a personality instead of a plain score. The words do as much work as the product.

All of this got tested on a real product with real users: the prompts behind the scoring, the copy behind "okayyy we see you," and the shipping that put it live.
